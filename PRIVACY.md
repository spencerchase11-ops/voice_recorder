# Privacy policy

*Voice Recorder* records audio only when you tap the record button, and it keeps
everything on your device.

- **Microphone.** The app uses the microphone only while you are recording.
  On Android, a notification is shown for as long as a recording is running.
- **Recordings.** Recordings are saved only on your device, in the folder
  you choose (Android) or in the app's folder in the Files app (iOS). The app
  sends them nowhere: they leave the device only when you share one yourself,
  through the system share sheet, with an app you pick, or with your phone's
  own backup (on iPhone the app's folder is part of iCloud and computer
  backups, like other apps' documents; a recording still in progress is
  left out).
- **Deleted recordings** stay on your device, hidden in the recordings
  folder, for 30 days (Settings → Recently deleted), then the app deletes
  them for good. If you uninstall the app before that, they stay in the
  folder, hidden; empty Recently deleted first to remove them.
- **Playback controls.** While a recording plays with "Lock screen controls"
  on, its name is shown on the lock screen and, on Android, in a
  notification. Only your device shows it.
- **Importing (iPhone).** "Import recordings" copies the files you pick in
  the Files app into the app's folder, on your device.
- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or
  tracking. It sends nothing to the developer or to third parties. On your
  device it stores your settings (recording type and quality, the chosen
  folder, sort order, playback speed, the other switches, and the last
  recording) and, to show the list quickly, the names of your recordings
  with the dates and lengths it read from them. Your phone's own backup
  (Google or iCloud), if you use it, may include the settings; the names,
  dates and lengths are left out of it.
- **Links.** "Rate 5 stars" (Android) and "Rate this app" (iPhone) open the
  app's page in Google Play or the App Store. Those stores' own privacy
  policies apply there.

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact address shown on the
app's Google Play or App Store listing.
